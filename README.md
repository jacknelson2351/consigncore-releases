# ConsignCore for Mac and Windows

ConsignCore in its own window on your computer. It is the same ConsignCore you use in
your browser: it opens the live site, so it is always up to date, and you sign in with
the same Google account.

The app keeps working with no internet connection: you can ring up sales and download
the sales file offline. When the connection is back and you are signed in, those sales
upload by themselves.

## Download

Get the app from the Download page on the ConsignCore website:

**https://kids-closet-six.vercel.app/download**

The files are also attached to the latest release on this page:

- `ConsignCore-mac.dmg` for Mac (macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel)
- `ConsignCore-windows.exe` for Windows 10 and 11

## How to install it

Your computer will show a warning the first time, because the app is not yet signed with
a paid certificate from Apple or Microsoft. The warning does not mean something is wrong
with the file. Follow the steps for your computer. You do this once for each download.

### On a Mac

1. Open **ConsignCore-mac.dmg** from your Downloads folder.
2. In the window that opens, drag **ConsignCore** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open your **Applications** folder and double-click **ConsignCore**. macOS says it
   could not verify the app. Click **Done**. Do not click Move to Trash.
4. Open the Apple menu and choose **System Settings**. Click **Privacy & Security** and
   scroll down to **Security**.
5. Next to the message about ConsignCore, click **Open Anyway**. The button is there for
   about an hour after step 3. If you do not see it, do step 3 again.
6. Click **Open Anyway** once more if asked, then enter your Mac password.

ConsignCore opens. From now on it opens with a double-click, like any other app.

### On Windows

1. Your browser may say the file is not commonly downloaded. In Microsoft Edge, point at
   the download, click the three dots (**More actions**), choose **Keep**, then
   **Show more**, then **Keep anyway**. In Chrome, click **Keep**.
2. Open **ConsignCore-windows.exe** from your Downloads folder.
3. A blue box says **Windows protected your PC**. Click **More info**, then
   **Run anyway**.

ConsignCore installs for your Windows account and opens. It adds a shortcut to the
desktop and the Start menu. No administrator password is needed.

## Signing in

In the app, click **Sign in with your browser**. Your browser opens, you sign in with
Google as usual and click **Sign in to the app**, and the app is signed in. Google does
not allow sign-in inside an app's own window, which is why your browser opens.

## Updates

The app shows the live website, so new features arrive by themselves. Now and then the
app itself gets a new version; it tells you when one is ready and opens the Download
page. Install it over the old one the same way.
